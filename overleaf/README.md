The Literature Review and the original H1--H4 Results material use "large
trade" (a market-specific relative-size classification), not wallet-level
language. "Whale" appears elsewhere only as a formally defined secondary
construct (the past-only whale-wallet proxy, WH1--WH4,
Section~\ref{sec:whale-wallet-design}) and is never used to relabel the
primary P99 large-trade treatment; `audit_v3_empirical_chapter.py` checks
that distinction directly rather than banning the word outright. The
supplementary market-integrity research question is represented by analyses
MI1--MI3 to avoid confusing it with the H1--H4 hypothesis numbering.

The Results chapter opens with a chapter-purpose paragraph and a
descriptive-statistics subsection (`table_summary_statistics.tex`,
`table_correlation_matrix.tex`), proceeds through the H1--H4 hypothesis tables
with economic-magnitude prose after each, and closes with a consolidated
robustness subsection and a `table_hypothesis_decisions.tex` summary table.
It embeds two figures from `figures/` (`fig_1_horizon_large_vs_ordinary.pdf`,
`fig_2_h4_timing_large_vs_ordinary.pdf`). Table numbering is automatic via
`\ref`/`\label`, so inserting new tables or figures anywhere in `results.tex`
renumbers everything correctly; do not hardcode table or figure numbers in
the prose.

The supplementary market-integrity methodology and results are maintained in separate included
files:

```tex
% Included at the end of sections/research_design.tex
\input{sections/rq3_market_integrity_methodology}

% Included at the end of sections/results.tex
\input{sections/rq3_market_integrity_results}
```

The latter calls `tables/table_rq3_integrity_results.tex`. These additions
cover concentration and price-update incidence (MI1), reversal behaviour
(MI2), out-of-sample prediction (MI3), anomaly screening, and the
signed contextual audit. An anomaly flag is treated as a screening result,
not proof of manipulation or market-maker identity.

The current tables use only standard LaTeX `table` and `tabular` environments
and do not require a table-specific package. `main.tex` additionally requires
`graphicx` (figures), `booktabs`, `natbib`, `hyperref`, and `caption`.

The final v3 large-trade tables are generated reproducibly with
`scripts/build_overleaf_v3_tables.py`; the descriptive-statistics and
correlation-matrix tables are generated by `scripts/build_v3_descriptive_stats.py`;
the two coefficient-plot figures are generated by
`scripts/build_v3_coefficient_plots.py`. Edit or rerun the relevant generator
rather than manually changing numerical results in the `.tex` files or the
figure files. The older `build_overleaf_regression_tables.py` belongs to the
preserved v1 high-activity-wallet analysis and must not be used to regenerate
the final v3 chapter.
