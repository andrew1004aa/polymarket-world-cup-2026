\subsection{Continuous cumulative-volume robustness for the whale-wallet analysis}
\label{sec:continuous-wallet-volume-appendix}

Section~\ref{sec:whale-wallet-results} reports the percentile-based WH1--WH4 decisions. As a threshold-free robustness check, whale status is replaced with the within-day standardised log of each wallet's cumulative gross USDC observed strictly before that day, and the same four sequence and price outcomes are re-estimated with this continuous score and its interaction with large-trade status, jointly Holm-adjusted across six focal tests.

Among ordinary actions, a one-standard-deviation increase in prior wallet scale is associated with a 0.1181 higher probability of another action within five minutes, a 0.0353 higher conditional same-direction probability, and a 0.0602 higher probability of an opposite action within 60 minutes (all $p<0.001$); prior cumulative gross trading volume therefore has a continuous activity gradient rather than an association confined to a top-one-per-cent cutoff.

The large-by-wallet-scale interactions for these three sequence outcomes are negative ($-0.0605$, $-0.0343$, and $-0.0395$; all Holm-adjusted $p<0.001$): higher-scale wallets are more active, but the large--ordinary difference narrows with wallet scale. By contrast, conditional favourable opposite execution has a positive interaction of 0.0191 (Holm-adjusted $p=0.0151$), and price-update incidence has a positive interaction of 0.00678 (Holm-adjusted $p=0.0309$). The directional-price interaction is small and negative ($-0.000027$, Holm-adjusted $p=0.0341$). These results reinforce the activity and favourable-execution components of WH2 and WH4 but do not support a uniformly positive large-by-wallet-scale price synergy; WH3 remains unsupported under this continuous specification, consistent with the percentile-based result in Section~\ref{sec:whale-wallet-results}.
